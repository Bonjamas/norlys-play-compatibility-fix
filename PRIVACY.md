# Privacy Policy

**Norlys Play Compatibility Fix does not collect personal data.**

The extension:

- does not collect or store browsing history;
- does not collect Norlys account information;
- does not collect credentials, cookies, payment information, or viewing history;
- does not send analytics or telemetry;
- does not communicate with a developer-controlled server;
- does not sell or share user data.

Its functionality is limited to changing browser-identification values for requests and page scripts on `norlysplay.dk` and its subdomains so that the site receives a Chrome-compatible browser identity.

The extension's host access is limited to:

- `https://norlysplay.dk/*`
- `https://*.norlysplay.dk/*`

If the extension's behavior changes in a future release in a way that affects data handling, this policy will be updated before that release is published.
